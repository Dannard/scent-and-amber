## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are fragrance shoppers evaluating products before purchase on both mobile and desktop.
They need to understand scent notes, wearing profile, and size/price quickly with minimal friction.
Core jobs: discover scents by family/mood/occasion, compare options, and complete checkout smoothly.

### Brand Personality
Refined and intimate.
The interface should feel modern, elevated, and quietly confident rather than loud or trend-driven.
Emotional goals: confidence, desire, and calm clarity during product evaluation.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode first, with support for a light theme toggle as a secondary mode.
Visual tone: editorial luxury with restrained warmth, rich spacing, and product-forward imagery.
Avoid generic, template-like ecommerce visuals and repetitive card-heavy patterns.

### Design Principles
1. Product-first clarity: scent profile, notes, and size/price hierarchy must be obvious early.
2. Quiet luxury execution: refined typography, controlled accents, and deliberate spacing over visual noise.
3. Mobile conversion focus: thumb-friendly controls, clear steps, and low-friction cart-to-checkout flow.
4. Honest trust design: no fabricated claims, testimonials, or misleading performance language.
5. Themed consistency: dark-first identity with a coherent, not gimmicky, light theme counterpart.

### Accessibility Baseline
Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic structure, and readable interaction states.

### Motion Direction
Allow richer editorial transitions, but keep motion intentional and non-distracting.
Use motion to reinforce hierarchy and state changes, not decorative over-animation.
